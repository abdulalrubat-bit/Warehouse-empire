# Warehouse Empire 2.9 (v20)

Two cuts below. Use the first if 2.7 and 2.8 already went out, so players are
coming from 2.8. Use the second if the last thing they received was 2.6, which
is what the Play console showed as the live release when 2.7 was being

## A. Coming from 2.8 — Play Store release notes (short)

A Priority Client now calls once a day with a high-value load. Fill it from
your normal output, then choose when to sign — hold it until that manifest's
market turns Hot and it pays considerably more.

Miss a day and your streak survives: one grace day a week, no questions.

Checking in now advances construction on your site, so a daily visit moves
something along even if you spend nothing.

## B. Coming from 2.6 — Play Store release notes (short)

Your site now runs in daylight, with racking that fills as you grow and a yard
busy with trailers and crew. Six new liveries to unlock, each reskinning the
whole app.

Markets now move — every manifest swings between Low, Normal, Hot and Peak.

New: a daily Priority Client, Crates currency, four more corporate buildings,
away reminders, and a streak that forgives a missed day.

## Longer version (for a store listing update or changelog page)

**A Priority Client, once a day.** A single high-value job, separate from the
contract board. It fills from your normal output, but you sign for it by hand
— and the card shows what that client's manifest is doing on the market. Sign
straight away, or hold the finished load until the market turns Hot and take
considerably more for it. It pays in cash and a Crate.

**Markets move.** Every manifest now runs its own market, swinging between
Low, Normal, Hot and Peak on its own clock. Peak is rare and brief, so
catching one is worth something. What you choose to haul is now a live
decision rather than something you set once and forget, and contracts written
during a good market keep that value until you fill them.

**Crates.** Liveries have their own currency now, earned from shipments and
the occasional daily bonus rather than competing with pallets for your perks.

**Four more corporate buildings.** A Customs Office that cuts contract quotas,
a Marketing Office that stretches good markets out, a Training Centre that
makes your site managers considerably stronger, and a Fleet Depot that takes
another bite out of equipment costs. Each has its own mastery at level ten.

**A streak that forgives.** Miss a day and you keep your run — one grace day a
week, refilling on its own schedule. You'll be told when it's used, so a
surviving streak doesn't look like a glitch.

**Daily visits build something.** Checking in advances construction on
whichever corporate plot is furthest behind, taking a few percent off its next
level and putting scaffolding up on the site. Days where you spend nothing
still move something forward.

**Fixed:** the daily stipend could be missed entirely if you left the game
open overnight rather than reopening it. It now arrives whether the app was
running or not.
