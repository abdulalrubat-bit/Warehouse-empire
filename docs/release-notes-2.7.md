# Warehouse Empire 2.7 (v17)

## Play Store release notes (short)

Your site now runs in daylight — full colour, real racking that fills as you
grow, and a yard busy with trailers, crew and freight.

Six new liveries to unlock: Hi-Vis Lime, Night Shift, Cold Chain, Radioactive,
Biohazard and Blackout. Each one reskins the whole app, not just the view.

New: reminders while you're away, with a switch for each kind. New: graphics
quality setting, or leave it on Auto.

Fixed: very large numbers now display properly, and a damaged save no longer
breaks the game.

## Longer version (for a store listing update or changelog page)

**The site view, rebuilt.** Daylight, painted colour and a lot more detail.
Your warehouse now reflects what you've actually built: racking fills in as
the fleet grows, the yard fills with trailers, pallets, crew and site
furniture, and the whole scene is lit rather than flat.

**Seven liveries.** Safety Orange plus six to unlock — Hi-Vis Lime, Night
Shift, Cold Chain, Radioactive, Biohazard and Blackout. Each is a distinct
palette and mood, three of them running the site at night, and each one
themes the entire app.

**The interface** has been redone in a light industrial style to sit with the
new site view.

**Away reminders.** Four kinds, each with its own switch in the Office: storage
full, daily stipend ready, a contract about to expire, and an overtime shift
available again. Everything is worked out on your device — nothing is sent
from a server.

**Milestone rewards.** Pallet bonuses at two career milestones.

**Graphics quality.** Low, Medium, High, or Auto, which measures your device
and adjusts as it goes.

**Fixes.**
- Numbers past a quintillion now display properly instead of running off the
  end of the scale.
- A damaged save file no longer leaves the game unplayable.
- Saves now live in device storage rather than browser storage, so clearing
  the app cache no longer risks your progress.
